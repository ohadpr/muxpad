Round 5 — wide, and with no edge to be inside of

Everything so far lived in a little box: a disc, a 26px square, a glyph slot. These are 90–120px wide and have no boundary at all — each one fades to nothing at its own edges rather than stopping at one, so there is no shape containing it and nothing reads as a widget. They sit in the row like weather, not like a control.

01Drift

Round 4's moiré, taken out of its disc and spread across 120px — then masked so its own intensity falls to zero in every direction. There is no circle, no rectangle, no edge: the interference simply thins out and stops existing, and the swirls wander across the band on a 13s/17s beat that never lands twice.

Still two divs and two keyframes. No JS, no SVG, no canvas — the cheapest thing in five rounds, and now the least contained.

03Aurora

Three blurred ribbons of light at different periods, sliding and stretching through each other, masked to nothing at both ends. No line, no dot, no discernible object at all — just coloured light that happens to be brighter in some places.

The only one here that is not monochrome, which is either the point or the problem.

04Wind

Fourteen short streaks riding a slowly-turning flow field, fading in and out as they travel so the field has no beginning and no end. It reads as a current you are looking at a piece of — the most literally "unconstrained" reading of the brief.
